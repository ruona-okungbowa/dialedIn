# 🎯 Dialed In - Find Your Wavelength

> A daily spectrum challenge game built for Reddit using Devvit

**Are you on the same wavelength as Reddit?** Test your mental frequency by placing words on spectrums between two opposites. Compete on leaderboards, build streaks, and see how you compare to the hive mind!

## 🎮 What is Dialed In?

Dialed In is a daily social guessing game built for Reddit, where players try to align their intuition with the community's consensus on a spectrum of opinions. It's a game of social calibration, designed to spark conversation and reveal the hidden patterns of the hive mind.

Each day presents a fresh challenge with 3 rounds of spectrum puzzles. Players use an interactive dial interface to position their guess on a visual spectrum arc, then see how they compare to the target position and the Reddit community average.

### For Reddit Users:

Daily Fun: A fresh, thought-provoking game every day that's quick to play and easy to understand.
Community Connection: See how your opinions align with the broader community, sparking friendly debates and shared experiences.
Interactive & Engaging: More than just a game, it's a daily social experiment that encourages active participation and discussion in comments.

### Key Features

- 🎯 **Daily Challenges**: 3 fresh spectrum rounds every day at midnight UTC
- 🏆 **Dual Leaderboards**: Weekly champions (resets Monday) and all-time top scorers
- 🔵 **Reddit Hive Mind**: Compare your guesses to the community average in real-time
- 🔥 **Streak System**: Build daily streaks and climb the ranks
- 🧪 **Spectrum Lab**: Submit your own spectrum ideas with sample clues
- 👮 **Moderator Panel**: Mods review submissions and add 3 clues with target positions

## 🎲 How to Play

1. Check the Daily Spectrum: Every day, a new spectrum is revealed. This could be anything from "Underrated" to "Overrated," or "Needs a Sequel" to "Should Have Been a Standalone."
2. Receive Your Clues: You will be given three clues related to the daily spectrum.
3. Place Your Guesses: For each clue, use the dial to place it on the spectrum where you think the community consensus lies. It's not about your personal opinion, but how well you can predict the hive mind!
4. View Your Results: After placing all three clues, you'll see your score. The closer your guesses are to the community average, the higher your score.
5. Share and Discuss: Share your results to the comments and see how you stack up against the rest of the community. This is where the real fun begins!
